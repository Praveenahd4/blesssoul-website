Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with BlessSoul?

Download BlessSoul from the App Store or Google Play and tap Continue as Guest — that's it. You can create an account later (email, Google, or Sign in with Apple on iPhone) to keep your data when you change phones. BlessSoul is free to use, with a free tier that includes a manifestation, goals, habits, Soul Spark quotes and daily scheduling. Premium is optional: it lifts the free limits and unlocks features such as the Soul Awakening journey, the Soul Portal calendar, sleep affirmations and schedule templates. You can see current plans and prices in the app before you buy.

What's the difference between free and Premium?

The free tier is genuinely usable: 1 active manifestation, 2 goals, 3 habits, 5 followed quote topics, 5 schedule blocks a day and the 369 and 5x55 methods. Free also includes AI help: 3 manifestations created with AI suggestions and 5 affirmation suggestions per manifestation and method. Premium removes the limits and adds all eight methods, Soul Awakening, habit progress history, the weekly and monthly review with PDF, quote history and topic exploration, the Soul Portal calendar, sleep affirmations, ready-made Soul Plans and a bigger AI allowance. If Premium ends, nothing you made is deleted — anything over a free limit simply becomes read-only until you resubscribe.

What happens if my subscription expires?

You keep your account and everything you created, and carry on using BlessSoul on the free tier. Nothing is deleted because a subscription ends — anything above the free limits is locked, not removed, and comes back if you resubscribe. See our Terms of Service for details.

Does Premium work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Premium belongs to your BlessSoul account, so sign in to the same account on either phone and it's there. A guest account stays on the device where it was created — create an account first if you want to use another device. Billing always stays with the store you bought through: manage, cancel or get a refund for an iPhone subscription through Apple, and for an Android one through Google Play.

How do I get a copy of my data?

You can export your goals and habits yourself, free: Settings → Data & Privacy → Export Goals & Habits (CSV). Premium members can also save their weekly or monthly review as a PDF. For a copy of everything else, email support@blesssoul.com from your registered email address with the subject "Data Request", and we'll send you a copy of your data in a common file format you can open and reuse, normally within 30 days.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete it yourself from inside the app at any time — or, if you can't open the app, ask us by email. See Deleting Your Account below for both.

I signed in with Apple or Google — what's my password?

You don't have one. If you created your account with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, there's no BlessSoul password to reset — just tap the same button you used originally and Apple or Google will sign you in. "Forgot Password?" only applies to accounts created with an email address and password.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app, go to Settings → Manage Subscription. Or, on iPhone: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → BlessSoul → Cancel Subscription; on Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → BlessSoul → Cancel subscription. You can cancel anytime, and you'll still have access to premium features until your current billing period ends.

Is my data secure?

Yes! Your data is transmitted securely over HTTPS and stored on trusted cloud infrastructure. We never sell your personal information. Your manifestations, goals, and personal information are securely stored and protected.

Does BlessSoul work offline?

Yes! You can use most features offline, including viewing and creating goals, tracking manifestations, and browsing quotes. Your data will automatically sync to the cloud when you're back online.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and enter your email address. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it right away.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your BlessSoul account and all of its data yourself, from inside the app on iPhone or Android. You don't need to email us or ask permission.

How to delete it

  1. Open BlessSoul and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Data Management.
  3. Under Danger Zone, tap Delete Account.
  4. If your account has an email or Apple/Google sign-in, sign in again to confirm it's really you.
  5. Confirm. Deletion happens immediately.

Deleting your account does NOT cancel your subscription.

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, and we cannot cancel them for you. If you have an active subscription, cancel it yourself, or the store will keep charging you even though your account is gone:

iPhone: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → BlessSoul → Cancel Subscription
Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → BlessSoul → Cancel subscription

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account: your profile, goals, milestones and progress, habits, manifestations and practice entries, schedules and Soul Plans, your Soul Awakening journey and Soul Record, any voice notes, your profile picture, quote history and favourites, and your sign-in credentials. Data on your device is cleared too. This is permanent and cannot be undone — we cannot recover a deleted account.

A small subscription record is kept with your email and name removed, so we can meet tax obligations and prevent repeated abuse of free trials. It holds none of your content and is no longer linked to an account. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

Want a copy of your data first?

Email support@blesssoul.com from your registered address with the subject "Data Request" before you delete. Once the account is gone we can't retrieve anything.

Can't open the app?

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, email support@blesssoul.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete the account and everything listed above, and confirm by email within 30 days. A guest account has no email address attached, so it can only be deleted from inside the app.

We never delete an account on our own because a subscription ends — only when you ask.

Troubleshooting

My data isn't syncing between devices

Check you're signed into the same account on both devices — this is by far the most common cause, especially if one device used Sign in with Apple and the other used email. Then confirm you have a connection: changes made offline are queued on the device and only upload once you're back online, so a device that has been offline will look out of date on your other devices until it reconnects. Open the app on the offline device and give it a moment to catch up. If a specific item still won't appear after both devices have been online, email us with the item's name.

My reminders and notifications stopped appearing

BlessSoul's reminders are scheduled on your device rather than pushed from a server, so the cause is almost always a phone setting. Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → BlessSoul (on Android, Settings → Apps → BlessSoul → Notifications, and allow Alarms & reminders so they arrive on time) and make sure notifications are allowed. Then check whether a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode is filtering them, and that Scheduled Summary isn't holding them until later in the day. Inside the app, confirm the reminder is still switched on for that goal or block. On Android, battery optimisation can also delay reminders — set BlessSoul to Unrestricted under Settings → Apps → BlessSoul → Battery. If you have a very large number of reminders set, try turning off ones you no longer need — iOS limits how many any single app can have scheduled at once, and the oldest can be dropped.

My widget or Live Activity isn't updating

Your phone decides when widgets refresh, based on your usage and the device's power budget — apps can request an update but cannot force one, so a widget may lag behind the app by a while. This is normal. Opening BlessSoul usually prompts a refresh. If a widget is stuck for a long time, remove it from your Home Screen and add it again, and check that Background App Refresh is enabled in iPhone Settings → General (on Android, that BlessSoul's battery use isn't restricted). Low Power Mode and Battery Saver significantly reduce widget updates.

AI affirmations aren't generating

AI features need an internet connection, so check that first. If you've been generating a lot in quick succession, you may have hit our rate limit — it's 20 requests per minute per account, to keep costs sustainable. Wait a minute and try again. Regenerating an affirmation has an allowance for each manifestation and method; when a free account has used it up, you'll see an upgrade prompt rather than an error. Goal setup has its own daily limit, and once it's reached you can still set the goal up yourself. If it still fails after a minute on a good connection, email us and mention roughly when it happened so we can find it in the logs.

My password reset email hasn't arrived

Check your spam and promotions folders first — automated emails often land there. Make sure you entered the same address you registered with; if you enter an address that has no account, no email is sent. If you signed up with Apple or Google, there is no password to reset — use that same sign-in button instead. Note that Sign in with Apple may have created your account with a private relay address rather than your personal one. If nothing arrives after 15 minutes, email us from the address you're trying to recover.

I'm being charged but don't have Premium

Go to Profile → Restore Purchases (also available in Settings, and via Restore at the bottom of the plan screen). This re-links a subscription you already own — useful after reinstalling or switching devices. Restore only works with the same Apple ID (on Android, the same Google account) that made the original purchase. If you're still charged without access after restoring, email us with the date of purchase and we'll look into it. iPhone refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; Android refunds go through Google Play, or email us with your order number.

The app crashes or won't open

Force-close BlessSoul and reopen it, then make sure you're on the latest version from the App Store or Google Play — crashes are often already fixed in an update. Restarting your phone clears a surprising number of one-off issues. If it keeps crashing, reinstalling is safe for your content: your goals, manifestations, schedule and profile picture are stored in your account and come back when you sign in. When you email us, telling us what you were doing right before the crash helps enormously.
