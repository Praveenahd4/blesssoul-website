How it works

Manifestation you practise,
not just wish.

One intention, carried through the day. You plant it, you act on it, and the universe gives it a rhythm.

  1. Intention
  2. Action
  3. Rhythm
  4. Depth
  5. Encouragement
01 · Intention the seed

Plant one clear intention.

Choose one of eight classic methods: 369, 5×55, scripting, gratitude, pillow, two cup, water or mirror work. BlessSoul helps you write the affirmation in your own words, then keeps the count, the times of day and every cycle.

  • Soul Manifest
  • 8 classic methods
  • Affirmations in your own words
02 · Action the work

Then give it a habit, a goal and a place in your day.

For each intention, BlessSoul suggests one daily habit and one measurable goal. Block time for them in your schedule, and keep the block you are in at a glance, even with the app closed. Done simply means you showed up.

Goals & HabitsSoul Schedule
03 · Rhythm the timing

Let the moon set the pace.

Moon phases, eclipses, retrogrades and number portals in one calendar. A message to start each morning, and a sleep ritual to close each night.

  • Soul Portal
  • Soul Sleep
  • Moon phase calendar
04 · Depth the inner work

Go deeper, one chakra a day.

Soul Awakening is a seven-day journey from Root to Crown, with breathwork, honest questions and an offering in your own words. One day opens each day, and everything you write is kept in your Soul Record.

  • Soul Awakening
  • Guided audio
  • Your Soul Record
05 · Encouragement the voice

Words that keep you going.

Soul Spark brings encouraging quotes from 12 topics and 118 subtopics, from Calm Down to Hard Times, shaped by what you follow. In the app when you open it, and on your Home Screen when you don't.

  • Soul Spark
  • 12 topics, 118 subtopics
  • Home Screen widget

    Why BlessSoul exists
    We never give up on our dreams. But somewhere along the way, we stop, and forget why we started.BlessSoul is built for that moment: to keep you on track, working on your manifestation every day, not just wishing for it.

    I know, because it happened to me. I'd set an intention, feel good about it, and two days later my actions had quietly stopped. Then I was back to just dreaming.

    Saying affirmations wasn't enough either, while what I did each day wasn't aligned with what I wanted. Belief, practice, a positive mindset and steady action all have to line up, and you have to keep going, without gaps.

    So I built one place that holds the whole cycle: dream, manifest, act, and stay on track, every day, until you get there. That's BlessSoul.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Anything else? Write to support@blesssoul.com. A person reads every message.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Tap “Continue as Guest” and start. You can add an account later so your practice is backed up and survives a new phone.

    Which manifestation methods are included?

    Eight: 369, 5×55, scripting, gratitude, the pillow method, two cup, water manifestation and mirror work. Each keeps its own counts, timing and cycle history.

    Is BlessSoul religious?

    No. It draws on manifestation, astrology and chakra traditions, and you choose what fits. In Settings you can tell Soul Spark about your beliefs and zodiac sign so the quotes you see suit you.

    Does BlessSoul promise results?

    No, and it never will. BlessSoul gives you a practice, a plan and good timing. What you do with the day is yours. It is not therapy or medical care.

    What happens to my data?

    Your manifestations, practice and goals are stored securely, never sold, and deleted completely when you delete your account in Settings. Read the Privacy Policy.

    Is it on Android or iPad?

    BlessSoul is made for iPhone today. The Android app is in final testing; share blesssoul.com/get and it will send people to the right store on launch day.
