## How to Activate on Launch Day

When Apple approves the app:

1. **Open `script.js`**
2. **Uncomment lines 95-122** (App Store activation code)
3. **Add your App Store URL:**
   ```javascript
   const APP_STORE_URL = 'https://apps.apple.com/app/blesssoul/YOUR_APP_ID';
   ```
4. **Commit and push to GitHub**
5. **Website auto-deploys** (GitHub Pages)

That's it! Takes 2 minutes.
